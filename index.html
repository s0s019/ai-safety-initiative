Mission & Vision

Global South Representation in Frontier AI Governance

As frontier AI models rapidly advance, ensuring their alignment, security, and beneficial deployment requires diverse global perspectives. Currently, Global South representation in frontier AI risk research and policy frameworks remains minimal.

The AI Safety & Governance Initiative bridges this gap by cultivating top student talent to contribute directly to global AI alignment standards, technical guardrails, and international strategy.

Technical Alignment

Model evaluation, red-teaming, and safety guardrails.

Policy & Governance

International treaties, compute limits, and policy frameworks.

Academic Curriculum

Curriculum Blueprint

A structured cohort curriculum combining machine learning fundamentals with technical safety, alignment, and global AI governance.

PHASE 1

Capabilities & Elicitation

Deep dive into foundation model architecture, LLM capabilities, emergent behaviors, and agentic workflows.

  • Foundation Models & Scaling
  • Agentic Tool Use & Elicitation
  • Capability Benchmark Analysis
PHASE 2

Risks & Misalignment

Analyzing systemic threat models, loss of control scenarios, situational awareness, and emergent misalignment.

  • Specification Gaming & Deception
  • Model Evaluations & Awareness
  • Loss of Control Threat Vectors
PHASE 3

Security & Guardrails

Technical defenses, prompt injection mitigation, red-teaming methodologies, model auditing, and bio-security.

  • Adversarial Red-Teaming
  • Cyber & Biological Defense
  • Model Auditing Standards
PHASE 4

Governance & Capstones

Exploring international treaties, middle-power strategy, compute governance, and fellow capstone projects.

  • International Policy & Strategy
  • Compute Limits & Regulation
  • Capstone Research Projects
